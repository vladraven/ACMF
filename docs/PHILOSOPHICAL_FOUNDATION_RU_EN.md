# Philosophical Foundation of ACMF 3.3.1.2

ACMF views civilization as an adaptive metabolic system: a self-maintaining process that must continuously reproduce people, trust, institutions, knowledge, resources, meaning, and collective agency.

## What ACMF is not

ACMF is not a prophecy, ideology, deterministic collapse model, or imported futurist cycle. It does not hard-code 240-year cycles or fixed scenario probabilities.

## Core philosophical claim

A civilization survives when it transforms complexity into adaptive capacity faster than complexity destroys its own foundations.

## Adaptive Dynamics extension

ACMF 3.3.1.2 adds a new conceptual layer:

```text
States -> Dynamics -> Criticality -> Phase Transition Probability -> Forecast
```

Regime change is interpreted as an endogenous consequence of criticality, not as a calendar event.

## Final statement

A civilization does not survive because it avoids entropy. It survives because it continuously creates enough order, knowledge, cooperation, trust, and meaning to overcome it.
